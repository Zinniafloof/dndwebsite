# The Village of Gourdvale

Welcome to Gourdvale! This is a quiet, isolated little farming spot. In the center of the village, you'll find the **Village Chief's House**, a local **Chapel**, a cozy little **Inn**, the main **Pumpkin Farms**, and two small **Villager Houses**.


![Gourdvalebirdview.jpg](/img/user/The%20Lost%20Echoes%20of%20Order/Locations/Location%20Pictures/Gourdvalebirdview.jpg)


In the small village of Gourdvale there is the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Gourdvale NPC's/Gourdvale Village Leadership#Village Chief\|Village chief]]'s House the Chapel, a small in, the pumpkin farm and 2 villager houses. 

### 🏠 The Gourdvale Patch Inn 

The local inn is currently run by **Lady Hermione Gourdpatch** with the help of her two younger siblings, Lisa and Fred. *DM Note: Their parents were recently killed by monsters, so the kids are holding the place down on their own.*


*Every dish is lovingly crafted from the valley's finest heirloom pumpkins and seasonal spices.*

| Dish | Cost | Description | Mechanical Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🥣 Roasted Gourd & Nutmeg Bisque** | 5 cp | A thick, velvety smooth pumpkin soup simmered with wild honey, toasted hazelnuts, and fresh nutmeg. Served inside a hollowed-out miniature gourd with a side of warm barley bread. | Gains **5 Temporary HP** after eating a full bowl. |
| **🥧 Spiced Pumpkin-Mash Pasty** | 8 cp | A golden, flaky pastry crust stuffed to the brim with seasoned pumpkin mash, sweet autumn apples, crumbled goat cheese, and sage. Easy to carry on the trail! | Gains **5 Temporary HP** after eating a full pasty. |
| **🍯 Candied Seed Brittle** | 3 cp | A pocket-sized cloth pouch filled with crunchy, wood-fired pumpkin seeds coated in a hard, caramelized maple glaze and sprinkled with coarse sea salt. | A pocket snack that can be eaten as a bonus action. |

### 🐄 The Fallwood Farm 

The local cow ranch is run by **Mrs. and Mr. Fallwood**. Mr. Fallwood is also the village merchant—he’s the one who travels to neighboring towns to bring back supplies and sell Gourdvale's famous pumpkins.
