A city in a flowery field at the edge of the Holy State of Stellarion, built as a memorial to the battle of the Lyrian River holds one of the three Keystones that seals away Alldrax in its main plaza where the Lyrian Cenopath is. 

The [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Aethelguard's NPC's/Marquis Valerius Lux\|Marquis Valerius Lux]] is in charge of the city, following the ruling philosophy of Phaedrax: Radiant perfection, Poise and Possesive protection.

## 📜 The Code of Memorial Decorum
*By order of [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Aethelguard's NPC's/Marquis Valerius Lux\|Marquis Valerius Lux]], the following four decrees are strictly enforced by the Sun-Glint Sentries to maintain the flawless purity of Aethelguard. Breaking a decree results in immediate heavy fines or detention in the castle cells.*



### ✨ 1. [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Locations/Aethelguard, Memorial City#The Decree of Silent Reverence (No Noise or Unsightliness)\|#The Decree of Silent Reverence (No Noise or Unsightliness)]]
*   **The Law:** No citizen or traveler may raise their voice, engage in rowdy behavior, or leave waste within the boundaries of the **Lyrian Cenotaph** plaza. 
*   **Roleplay Impact:** The players cannot shout, run, or stage loud arguments near the monument. If they are caught being loud or disrespectful, guards will immediately approach and demand they leave the high terrace.

### ⏳ 2. [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Locations/Aethelguard, Memorial City#The Decree of the Twilight Vault (No Wandering at Night)\|#The Decree of the Twilight Vault (No Wandering at Night)]]
*   **The Law:** The memorial plaza is entirely restricted to the public from midnight until the first rays of dawn pierce the valley. 
*   **Roleplay Impact:** This is your primary **Heist obstacle**. Walking into the circular plaza layout past midnight is an automatic criminal offense, forcing the players to rely strictly on stealth to outmaneuver the paladin patrol routes.

### 🧼 3. [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Locations/Aethelguard, Memorial City#The Decree of Impeccable Presentation (No Improper Dress or Odor)\|#The Decree of Impeccable Presentation (No Improper Dress or Odor)]]
*   **The Law:** No creature may enter the sacred upper tiers while unwashed, foul-smelling, or clad in bloodied, torn, or unrefined armor. 
*   **Roleplay Impact:** If the party tries to march straight from a muddy wilderness trail up to the plaza or castle, the guards at the residential checkpoint will block them. This forces a great roleplay scene where the characters must visit the **Lux & Trim Barbershop** or buy clean clothes from the **Celestial Boutique** just to get permission to walk up the road!

### 🎀 4. [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Locations/Aethelguard, Memorial City#The Decree of the Peace-Bound Hilt (The Fourth Rule)\|#The Decree of the Peace-Bound Hilt (The Fourth Rule)]]
*   **The Law:** No outsider may carry an open or unbound weapon within the city limits. All blades and arcane implements must be sealed at the entry gates with the city's official gold silk ribbon.
*   **Roleplay Impact:** Drawing a weapon or casting a hostile spell instantly snaps the gold thread. It acts as an automated magical alarm, letting out a loud chime like a cathedral bell that alerts all sentries within 120 feet.
