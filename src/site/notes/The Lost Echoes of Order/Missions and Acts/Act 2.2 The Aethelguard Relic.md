## Entering the Memorial City

Entering the Flowery paths of the beautiful prairies  of the Stellarion Kingdom a plain and tall wall of Quartzite stands over the Lyran River with Pure white gates and a picturesque bridge.

Two sunlight sentries stand in the entrance ready to check on the travelers that are eager to enter the city. The party can't be carrying stinky items or be dirty cause the guards will invoke the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Locations/Aethelguard, Memorial City#🧼 3. The Decree of Impeccable Presentation (No Improper Dress or Odor)\| The Decree of Impeccable Presentation]]

Once the party is accepted by the guards they are escorted by a different sentry to the adventurers guild to have their weapons tied with the magical silk ribbon to abide with the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Locations/Aethelguard, Memorial City#🎀 4. The Decree of the Peace-Bound Hilt (The Fourth Rule)\| The Decree of the Peace-Bound Hilt]] 

From here the party is free to explore the city as they like to gather information on the relic. the Locals will gladly inform them about the Memorial [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Locations/Aethelguard, Memorial City#Lyrian Cenopath\|Lyrian Cenotaph]] where the relic is located in remembrance of the Battle of the Lyrian River that they crossed to reach the city.

The party will have 2 options, either try to steal the relic or try to get an audience with the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Aethelguard's NPC's/Marquis Valerius Lux\|Marquis Valerius Lux]] and try to negotiate with him to get the relic.

## 1. The Phaedrian Deal

The party opts for the peaceful route where their aim is to get an audience with the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Aethelguard's NPC's/Marquis Valerius Lux\|Marquiss]] and receive the relic. For this they will have options as to how to obtain an audience:

#### High Society Sponsorship:

The players can encounter a local noble lady either in the Lyran Lounge or in the Seamstress shop in the Artisan road where they can befriend her to help them get in contact with the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Aethelguard's NPC's/Marquis Valerius Lux\|Marquiss]]. 

##### Execution:

Players can get prepped up in the local barbershop and get fine clothes in the boutique to present themselves to this noble lady and get in her favor. 

##### Challenge:

Need to pass a DC 12 Charisma check to have her set up an audience for the party but requires that the whole party needs to be properly dressed for the occasion


#### Obsidian Emergency

The players can try to approach a soldier a sentry and show proof of the corruption and the plans of the cultists of [[Obsidian Dawn\|Obsidian Dawn]] to obtain an audience with the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Aethelguard's NPC's/Marquis Valerius Lux\|Marquis]]. If this convinces the soldier he will go inform his captain and have the party await for an audience.

##### Execution:

Players will have to have looted the cultist's robes, have scales from the defeated beasts or if they come directly from [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Locations/The Azure Ruins\|The Azure Ruins]] present the relic the obtained from their previous battle.


#### Daily Checkpoint

The party tries to encounter directly with the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Aethelguard's NPC's/Marquis Valerius Lux\|Marquis]] during his weekly patrol of the city.

##### Execution:

[[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Aethelguard's NPC's/Marquis Valerius Lux\|Valerius]] is still a soldier at heart so even if he is a high noble he still goes around patrolling the city to keep it safe and splendid

##### Challenge: 

The party will have to be clean and look the best part while also carrying the corrupted relic obtained from [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Locations/The Azure Ruins\|The Azure Ruins]] to present as proof to the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Aethelguard's NPC's/Marquis Valerius Lux\|Marquis]] and pass a DC 16 Charisma check for persuasion and a DC 10 of insight to approach the entourage without breaking [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Locations/Aethelguard, Memorial City#✨ 1. The Decree of Silent Reverence (No Noise or Unsightliness)\|The Decree of Silent Reverence]] if approached in the memorial area.


#### The Audience with the Marquis

Once the party successfully obtains an audience they are escorted to the castle where the negotiation starts.

##### The Negotiation:

The party has to convince the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Aethelguard's NPC's/Marquis Valerius Lux\|marquis]] to lend them the relic.

*  The spokesperson must make a DC 15 Charisma check (persuasion) to explain the threat of the [[Obsidian Dawn\|Obsidian Dawn]] cult and convince him that not giving them the relic will result on the seal to burst and corrupt the city with obsidian scales
*  Modifiers: speaking in an eloquent way (roleplaying the speech in a fancy way) gives +1 to the roll. If the entire party is clean and properly dressed +2 to the roll. if someone in the party is dirty or smelly -2 to the roll.
*  **Failure**: If the offend the pride of the marquis they will be escorted out of the castle and will have to pivot to stealing the relic.
* **Success**: The marquis agrees to lending the relic but he wont lend it without commitment from the party and require a branding ritual

##### [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Loot and Inventory/Loot & rewards/Phaedrian Shackle\|Phaedrian Shackle]]

One player will have to submit themselves to the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Loot and Inventory/Loot & rewards/Phaedrian Shackle\|Phaedrian Shackle]] where the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Aethelguard's NPC's/Marquis Valerius Lux\|Marquis Valerius Lux]] will use his rapier to press onto the skin of the volunteer and a blinding light release and searing an emblem of the Stellarion kingdom forming the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Loot and Inventory/Loot & rewards/Phaedrian Shackle\|Phaedrian Shackle]].


## 2. The Heist 

If the party avoids the contract, or fails to secure an audience with the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Aethelguard's NPC's/Marquis Valerius Lux\|marquis]] they will have to steal the relic from the memorial plaza of the city.

### Phase 1:  Passing through the Residential Area

The party leaves the inn they are staying at breaking the [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Locations/Aethelguard, Memorial City#⏳ 2. The Decree of the Twilight Vault (No Wandering at Night)\|Decree of the Twilight Vault]]. the streets are silent and dimly lit since nobody dares to break the decree, specially outsiders so tehre isn't any patrols in the lower area.


In the residential area there are 3 sentries patrolling the streets.

* **Challenge**: the group has to pass a DC 12 Dexterity check (stealth)
* **Failure**: It alert the guards and forces them to go back to their inns unless they want to be expelled from the city.

### Phase 2: Reaching The Memorial

The party has to pass a DC 14 Dexterity check (stealth) to bypass the sentry in the stairs to wards the memorial and then a DC12 Dexterity check to by pass the 3 paladins patrolling the memorial. or they can try to distract the guards with other mediums.

Once they reach the actual statue they have to take the relic that is embedded in its slot with a magical seal.

* **Challenge**: The character that is going to extract the seal ahs to pass a DC 15 Intelligence check (arcana) to undo the magical seal
	* **Success**: the seal dissolves silently and the relic slides out of its slot into the players hand. they can slip away through a small alley towards the artisans road back to their rooms in the inn.
	* **Failure**: The seal shines and deals 2d4 radiant damage. if the played fails 3 times before the guards realize the party is taking the relic the seal starts chiming loudly alerting all the guards around.
