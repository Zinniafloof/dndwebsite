# The Blight of Gourdvale

## Act Overview:
The party arrives in a small rural village with pumpkin farms called [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Locations/Gourdvale\|Gourdvale]]. The [[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Gourdvale NPC's/Gourdvale Village Leadership#Village Chief\| Village Chief]] tasks them with fighting off the wildlife that has been infected with Black scales.

## Key NPCs and Locations:

[[The Lost Echoes of Order/Characters/NPC's/Gourdvale NPC's/Gourdvale Village Leadership#Village Chief\| Gourdvale Village Chief]]: Gives the quest to the party

[[Gourdvale Priest\|Gourdvale Priest]]: After the party comes back with the relic the priest analyzes it and informs them it has the emblem of the [[Platinum Dragon Luminisax\|Platinum Dragon Luminisax]] and writes them a recommendation letter to the [[The Seekers of Dawn\|The Seekers of Dawn]] Guild to inquire about it further.

## Investigation:

After Defeating the wildlife the party can investigate further into the forest to find the source of corruption.

**The Goal**: 3 Success Checks before 2 failures
**DC 12** for all investigation/survival checks

### Outcomes:

**Success:** Find the Keystone in a Hidden groove in the Forest being corrupted by the scales
**Failure**: Fight a group of 3 cultists that have the Keystone themselves and obtain it.
