## Village Chief

### Grandpa Tobias Greenwheel

A warm old man taking care of the village he grew up in. Now he is troubled by the mutating wildlife threatening his community.

He walks around with a old wooden crane that has a clumsily carved pumpkin in the top. always hench over and constantly adjusting his glasses, despite his apparent fragility he is a hardy man that works on the local pumpkin farms day in and day out.

## Village Priest

### Father Rowan Miller

A serene young man that came back from his religious studies to his birthplace to run the local chapel and aid the community. A devout follower of the [[Platinum Dragon Luminisax\|Platinum Dragon Luminisax]].

A rural burly man with priest robes, kind features and rough hands that see manual labor on a regular basis
